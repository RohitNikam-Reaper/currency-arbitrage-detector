CONCEPT

Why a negative cycle?

Currency conversion is multiplicative, while graph algorithms operate on additive edge weights.

r₁ × r₂ × r₃ > 1

Applying the negative logarithm transforms a profitable multiplication cycle into a negative-weight cycle.

COMPLEXITY

Bellman-Ford

O(VE)

V represents the number of currencies and E represents the number of directed exchange edges.

LIMITATIONS

Real-world costs

A detected cycle represents a potential opportunity. Transaction fees, spreads, slippage, liquidity and latency can affect actual profitability.
